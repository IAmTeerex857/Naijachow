# Health And Nutrition Disclaimer - Draft

NaijaPlate provides general meal-planning information, not medical advice. Nutrition values are estimates and can vary by ingredients, portions, brands, and preparation methods.

The service does not guarantee that meals are free from allergens or cross-contamination. Users with allergies, pregnancy-related needs, diabetes, kidney conditions, hypertension, or other medical concerns should follow advice from a qualified clinician and independently verify every ingredient.

Seek urgent professional help for a medical emergency. Do not delay or replace professional care based on content generated by NaijaPlate.
