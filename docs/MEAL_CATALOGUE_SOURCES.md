# Nigerian Meal Catalogue Sources

Naijachow meal candidates are explicit combinations rather than automatically pairing every carb with every protein.

Primary references:

- Nigerian cuisine overview: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerian_cuisine
- Akara serving traditions: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akara
- Moi Moi serving traditions: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moin_moin
- Amala and soup pairings: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amala_(food)
- Nigerian breakfast recipes: https://www.allnigerianrecipes.com/breakfast-recipes/
- Nigerian Jollof Rice accompaniments: https://www.allnigerianrecipes.com/rice/nigerian-jollof-rice/
- Nigerian soups and swallows: https://www.allnigerianrecipes.com/soups/

Selected foods are preference anchors. They rank matching verified meals higher but do not limit the complete plan to only those foods.
