# Naijachow Refund Policy - Draft

Effective date: To be confirmed

This document applies to one time Bachs payments for 30 days of Premium access and requires legal review before publication.

## Billing

Premium does not renew automatically. Access normally remains available until the end of the purchased 30 day period.

## Refund Requests

Users may request a refund for duplicate charges, unauthorized transactions, or a material service failure. Requests should include the account email and transaction reference but never full payment-card details.

## Review

Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method where supported. Processing time depends on Bachs and the user's financial institution.

## Exceptions

The final policy must specify any statutory cooling off rights, refund window, partial period treatment, promotional purchases, chargeback handling, and local consumer protection requirements.
